# Banking 2026–2028: Industry Landscape & Strategic Context

*Research briefing compiled Aug 2026. Sources at the bottom. This document
frames the compliance suite within the current regulatory and technology wave
so the project can be presented (portfolio, roadmap, or internal pitch) as
part of what the industry is moving toward — not as a standalone tool.*

---

## 1. The AI wave: from chatbots to governed agents

- **Agentic AI is the new operating layer.** Banks are moving from "AI as a
  feature" to fleets of specialized, governed AI agents doing end-to-end work
  (onboarding, credit analysis, disputes, compliance). Gartner: 17% of banking
  CIOs already run multi-agent systems, 41% plan deployment within 12 months;
  by end-2027 agents are expected to drive ~30% of day-to-day banking
  decisions. Oracle, Accenture, Finastra and Backbase all frame 2026 as the
  year AI goes from pilot to production scale.
- **Domain-specific financial models** — Gartner projects 60%+ of bank GenAI
  models will be industry-specific by 2028 (vs 30% in 2025), specifically to
  lower hallucination rates in compliance, fraud, and risk contexts.
- **Explainability is a product requirement now.** "The model says so" is no
  longer acceptable to supervisors. Banks must produce audit-ready evidence
  for every AI-assisted decision (EU AI Act Art. 13, ECB supervisory
  expectations, EBA guidelines) and keep humans able to override decisions
  that affect customers.
- **AI-driven fraud defense** must match AI-driven fraud offense — deepfake
  identity fraud and synthetic identities are now one unified threat surface.

## 2. The EU regulatory wave (the big one)

| Regime | Status (Aug 2026) | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EU AI Act — high-risk** (credit scoring, creditworthiness: Annex III) | Applies **2 Dec 2027** (deferred from Aug 2026 by the Digital Omnibus) | Risk-management system, bias testing, technical documentation, human oversight, FRIA before go-live; fines up to €35M / 7% of turnover |
| **PSD3 + Payment Services Regulation (PSR)** | Politically agreed Nov 2025; adoption expected 2026; most rules apply ~2028 | Replaces PSD2: merged PI/EMI licensing, **mandatory Verification of Payee (name↔IBAN)**, stricter open-banking API quality KPIs, stronger fraud obligations |
| **FiDA — open finance** | Expected ~2027 | Extends data sharing beyond accounts: mortgages, savings, investments, insurance (FDSS APIs) |
| **Instant Payments Regulation (SCT-Inst)** | Eurozone live (Oct 2025); **non-euro states incl. Denmark: receive Jan 2027, send Jul 2027** | 10-second, 24/7 instant transfers; **Verification of Payee + daily sanctions screening** become mandatory |
| **DAC8 / crypto-asset reporting** | In force from 2026 | RCASPs report 2026 data to tax authorities by Sep 2027 |
| **EU AML package (AMLR / AMLD6 / AMLA)** | Applies **10 Jul 2027** | Directly-applicable AML rulebook; UBO threshold 25%+; CASPs in scope; AMLA operational since Jul 2025 |
| **DORA** | In force since Jan 2025 | ICT resilience rules treat AI systems as active tech components (incident taxonomy, third-party oversight) |
| **MiCA** | Transitional period ended 1 Jul 2026 | Crypto-asset services need EU authorisation / passporting |

## 3. Denmark specifics

- **Nationalbank joined TARGET / T2 / TIPS with DKK (April 2025)** — first
  non-euro-area central bank in all three TARGET services. Danish krone
  instant settlement is live; the mandatory instant-payment deadlines for
  Danish institutions: **receive Jan 2027, send Jul 2027**.
- **AL Bank formed (Dec 2025)** — merger consolidating Danish mid-cap
  banking; more scale for digital investment while keeping the relationship-
  driven corporate/SME model.
- **Danske Bank** — Forward '28 strategy: heavy GenAI/agent investment, cloud
  migration, 20% tech-productivity target, AI hubs in Belfast/Warsaw.
  **Jyske Bank** — record results, expanding digital/AI initiatives and a
  stablecoin consortium. **BEC Financial Technologies** (shared IT provider
  for ~40 Danish banks, incl. Bank) is running a platform-modernisation
  programme — the next big API/AI uplift will flow through BEC.
- **MobilePay/Vipps merger** → Nordic platform with 11M+ users. Danish
  fintech's edge is trust + interoperability, not scale: green finance/ESG
  reporting, embedded finance, open finance are the natural next layer.

## 4. What Danish banks should adopt or upgrade

1. **Verification of Payee (name↔IBAN) now** — mandatory under PSD3/IPR;
   building it early is a fraud-prevention and cost differentiator.
2. **Citation-verified AI for credit analysis** — EU AI Act Art. 13: every
   AI-generated figure in a credit memo needs a machine-verifiable source.
3. **AI model inventory + FRIA pipeline** — high-risk obligations land
   Dec 2027; the audit-readiness program (inventory, classification,
   documentation, monitoring) takes months and starts now.
4. **Instant-payment readiness (TIPS)** — batch → 24/7 real-time processing;
   sanctions screening per payment, not per day.
5. **AML uplift for AMLR 2027** — transaction monitoring re-scoped, UBO
   25% threshold, crypto service providers in scope.
6. **Open-finance API strategy (FiDA)** — mortgage/savings/investment data
   APIs; embedded-finance and ESG-reporting plays for SME customers.
7. **Domain-specific financial LLMs + synthetic data** — privacy-compliant
   test data (what the anonymizer enables).

## 5. How this project fits the wave

- `core/verification.py` is a working reference implementation of EU AI Act
  **Art. 13 explainability** for AI-generated financial analysis: every metric
  and risk flag carries a page/table/row citation that is deterministically
  re-checked against the source PDF. The live demo (Novo Nordisk 2024 report,
  DeepSeek) proved the engine catches the LLM fabricating figures it never saw
  in context — exactly the hallucination story regulators worry about.
- `anonymizer/` maps to the **Verification of Payee / GDPR / synthetic-data**
  primitives: checksum-validated IBAN (ISO 7064 mod-97), Luhn card checks,
  CPR plausibility, and an audit trail — offline by design.
- Together they demo the two compliance priorities of 2026–2028: *explainable
  AI* and *data protection in sharing workflows*.
